# What Masroof Does
Masroof is an expense-management application. Employees submit and track expense requests. Managers review requests for their own department. Finance administrators oversee budgets, category limits, and organization-wide spending.

# Roles and Responsibilities
Employees can ask about their own expenses and applicable category limits. Managers can review expenses in their department, including textual risk explanations. Finance administrators can review organization-wide spending and department budgets.

# Expense Lifecycle
Expense statuses are Pending, Approved, and Rejected. The assistant is read-only and cannot create, edit, submit, approve, or reject expenses.
